# Controller Persona — Scratch Notes

Scope: defensive posture only. Other gaps (stakeholder graph, cadence asymmetry) deferred.

## Core reframe

- Real driver = **don't get caught**
- Controller is risk-averse career-protector, not workflow-optimizer
- Tools bought for *defensibility*, then for *speed*

## Restatement fear

- ASC 606 mods touch transaction price allocation across periods → high restatement risk
- Restatement = career event. Resume scar. Audit committee notice. SEC comment letter (if public)
- Backdated amendment is the canonical nightmare: numbers move after close started
- Cumulative catch-up adjustment is restatement's polite cousin — same evidence requirements
- Persona spends emotional energy hedging against the *future audit conversation*

## Audit defensibility as buying criterion

- "Can I show my work?" beats "Did I save time?"
- Workpaper standard: every number traceable to source, every judgment documented, every change versioned
- External auditor PBC list is the implicit spec for what good evidence looks like
- Diff artifact only valuable if it survives audit scrutiny — screenshot is not workpaper
- Close memo only valuable if cited to ASC 606 paragraphs, not vibes

## SOX 404 adjacency (pre-IPO track)

- Series B-C controllers often prepping for IPO readiness in 12-24 months
- Same mod-diff artifacts double as SOX control evidence
- Revenue is the highest-risk SOX cycle — any rev rec tool sits in audit scope
- Implication: tool must produce evidence that satisfies *both* GAAP audit and ITGC review
- Adjacent buyer: VP Controller / Director of SOX, not just Controller

## Product positioning shift

- Diff produces audit-ready evidence the moment a mod lands
- Workpaper for auditor, with CFO as secondary reader
- PBC bundle is the *primary* artifact, diff/memo are inputs to it

## Feature implications

- Every diff needs immutable timestamp + source-of-truth pointer (which sub-ledger version, which contract PDF revision)
- Close memo prompt should cite ASC 606-10 paragraphs, not narrate freely
- Memo should state ASC 606 accounting treatment and distinguish prospective vs cumulative catch-up revenue impact pattern explicitly
- PBC bundle should include audit trail log, not just final numbers

## FAQ

- Does the demo need a fake "auditor view" mode to dramatize defensibility? No
- Should close memo have explicit "materiality flag" on each mod? Yes
