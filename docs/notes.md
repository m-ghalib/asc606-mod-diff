# Design Notes

## Contract Data Model

1. Date
2. Length of the contract
3. Type of Contract (Master / Amendment)
4. Contracting Entity
5. Related Contracts
6. Products Covered
7. Clauses (which clauses are most important for a controller)
8. Pricing (scaling options / tapering)
9. Risks
10. ASC 606 Treatment (separate contract / termination plus new contract / modification of existing contract)
11. Revenue Impact Pattern (prospective / cumulative catch-up / allocation reshuffle)
12. Provenance IDs
13. Usage Meters
14. Invoice Lines
15. Revenue Schedule Lines
